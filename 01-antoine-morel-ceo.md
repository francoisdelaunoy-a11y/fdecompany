## Context
Fourth generation of the Morel family; CEO since 2019. Sixty minutes in his office in Lyon.

## What he said
- "In three years we lost almost a hundred million of revenue. Some of it is resin prices. Twenty-five thousand tonnes of it is real."
- "We are not short of capacity. We have nearly seventy thousand tonnes of it doing nothing. I don't want a project that makes more tonnes. Nobody is buying them."
- The bank covenant is his first worry: "On the budget we sit exactly at the limit. One bad quarter and the bank writes my strategy."
- The cost program: €15M of EBITDA by the end of 2027. "Purchasing and headquarters have a plan. Operations has six million and no plan."
- On AI: "My friends' companies all have pilots. None of them can show me a euro. I want to start where it shows in our accounts by December, and where Mireille's people can check it."
- On the footprint review: "The board will look at all four sites in June. I want to decide it on facts, not on who shouts the loudest."
- "We have never closed a plant in 102 years. That is not a promise. It is a record I would like to keep."

## What he asked us
One page, by the end of the month: which plant first, on which problem, what it is worth, and why not the others.
