## History

- 1924: Henri Morel starts making celluloid combs in Lyon.
- 1950s: moves into thermoplastics; the Lyon site becomes a compounding plant.
- 1962: sells the celluloid business, which was declining. The family still tells the story.
- 1978: opens Haguenau to supply the automotive industry in eastern France and Germany.
- 1991: opens Évreux for color concentrates (masterbatch).
- 2011: acquires a compounder in Tarragona, Spain, for Iberian packaging.
- 2015: launches the Polymex Way lean program, first at Haguenau.
- 2022: record revenue (€612M), driven by resin price inflation; Haguenau commissions line H5 (€38M) for an electric-vehicle program.
- 2024: the EV program is cancelled by the carmaker; H5 runs at about 30%.
- 2023 to 2025: three years of decline in revenue and volume.
