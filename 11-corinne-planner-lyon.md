## What she said
- "I build the plan on Friday from SAP and I rewrite it every day. Otherwise nothing would ship."
- "SAP says sixty minutes for every changeover. Black to natural is four hours. Natural to black is half an hour. SAP doesn't know the difference."
- "Light to dark when I can: natural, white, colors, black. The retail orders come in small and break everything."
- Friday evenings until 20:00; phone calls on Saturday.
- "Jean-Pierre retired in March. He and I were the only ones who knew the rules."
