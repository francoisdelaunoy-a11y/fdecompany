*From Antoine Morel to the board of Polymex Industries, 18 March 2026. Extract shared with the diagnostic team with his agreement.*

## Where we are

Revenue has fallen for three years, from €612M in 2022 to €515M in 2025. Part of it is resin prices coming down, which we pass on to customers. Part of it is real: we sold 25,000 fewer tonnes in 2025 than in 2022. Automotive is shrinking faster than we planned, and the cancelled EV program left Haguenau's new line mostly idle. Our customers in packaging are flat; retail is the only segment growing, and it wants smaller, faster deliveries than we are used to.

Our EBITDA was €48.3M in 2025. The budget for 2026 is €43.5M. Our bank covenant is net debt at no more than 3.5 times EBITDA, tested every 31 December. At €43.5M we would sit exactly at the limit, with no margin for a bad quarter.

## What I propose

1. **A cost program of €15M of EBITDA by the end of 2027.** Purchasing €6M (resin contracts, Olivier Lambert), overheads €3M (headquarters), operations €6M. The operations part does not yet have a plan or an owner.
2. **No growth capex in 2026 and 2027.** Only maintenance capex and the Évreux line approved in 2025. We have capacity we do not use; we will not buy more.
3. **A footprint review**, presented to this board in June. Our four plants run at 78% of their capacity on average. We owe our shareholders, our bank and our people a clear view of what each site is for.
4. **Artificial intelligence in operations**, with an outside team, to bring costs down. Start where it will show in our numbers by the end of the year, and only where our own controllers can measure it.

## What I do not want

More tonnes nobody has ordered. Another project whose gains we cannot find in the accounts. A plant that hears about its future from a rumor.
