## What she said
- The Polymex Way started at Haguenau in 2015 "because the carmakers demanded it". Haguenau scores 3.3 out of 4: "They run daily management better than our customers."
- "At Lyon we never got the foundations. We did a beautiful SMED workshop on M02 in 2019: minus 35% in two months. Then the crews rotated, the plan changed every day, and it was gone by Christmas."
- Tarragona: "Nothing. Paper and goodwill."
- Her budget was halved in 2024. "Lean has given what it can at Haguenau. Their problem is not waste, it is that there are no orders."
- On AI: "If it builds on standards, I am all for it. If it replaces them with a black box, the crews will ignore it within a month."
