## What she said
- "830 kilowatt-hours per tonne. Lyon is at 640. Our extruders date from 1998 to 2004 and the motors are oversized."
- "One meter for the site. Sub-meters per line would cost about €40K; it is in the maintenance budget for 2026."
- "Heaters stay on during long stops because nobody has the time to switch them off and restart."
- She estimates 10 to 15% of energy could be saved "if we knew where it goes".
