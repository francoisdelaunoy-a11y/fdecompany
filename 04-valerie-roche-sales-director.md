## Context
Sales director since 2018. Forty minutes, by video.

## What she said
- "Automotive is going down eight percent a year and I don't see the bottom. Packaging is flat. Retail is the only thing growing, and retail wants small lots in seven days."
- "In 2025 we lost twelve million of sales because the plants did not deliver."
- Codra Retail has put 30% of its volume with Lyon out to tender, about 5,160 t: "Nine million of revenue. If Lyon loses it, the 2026 budget is gone."
- Prices: commodities down about 1.5% a year; automotive customers ask for 3% a year.
- "We do not need more capacity. We need to deliver what we promise, where the customers still grow."

## Our notes
- The [lost-orders file](../sales/lost-and-at-risk-orders.md) shows €5.05M lost for delivery reasons at Lyon and €1.7M for lack of capacity at Évreux in 2025 and Q1 2026; the rest of the losses are price and programs. Her €12M mixes the three. To be reconciled with her team.
