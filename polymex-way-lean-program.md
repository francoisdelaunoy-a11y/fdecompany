| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 2015 | Launched at Haguenau, at the request of automotive customers: 5S, standard work, daily shift meetings |
| 2017 | Extended to Lyon and Évreux; group CI director appointed |
| 2019 | SMED workshop on Lyon line M02: changeover time down 35% in two months; not sustained after the planner and the crews changed |
| 2021 | TPM pilot at Haguenau; OEE becomes a group KPI |
| 2023 | Group OEE definition changed to calendar time, to show idle capacity |
| 2024 | Program budget cut by half |
| 2026 | No program at Tarragona yet |
