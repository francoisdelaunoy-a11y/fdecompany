## What he said
- SAP ECC everywhere; S/4HANA migration planned for 2027, "budget permitting".
- Data by plant: "Haguenau has AutoTrack, a real MES, because automotive requires traceability. Évreux has a small MES since 2021. Lyon has the LineLog exports every day since 2019, which are better than people think. Tarragona has paper, typed into Excel on Fridays."
- Any new cloud service goes through a group security review: about six weeks. Data must stay in the EU.
- Nadia at Lyon is his best site IT manager: "She will help you. She will not help you build another orphan."
- "Start where the data already exists, and leave something IT can support."
