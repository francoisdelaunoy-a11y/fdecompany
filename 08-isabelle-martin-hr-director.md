## What she said
- "The group has never made a collective layoff. People know it, and they know the footprint review is coming."
- Direct labour is a fixed cost in the short term in France and Spain: "You cannot plan a gain on fewer people. You can plan on overtime, temporary staff and people moving to better work."
- Lyon: average age 49; about 30% of the workforce retires within eight years, including two of the three most experienced planners and shift leaders.
- Any tool that could be used to monitor individual employees must be presented to the works council (CSE) before it is used.
- "If a tool changes someone's job, I want to know before the person does, and I want their manager to tell them."
