## Context
COO since 2021, previously plant manager at a German automotive supplier. Fifty minutes.

## What he said
- "Haguenau is at 51% OEE. That is the worst number in the group. Take it to 65% and you have fifteen thousand tonnes more."
- On Lyon: "Lyon is a planning problem. Hervé told me so himself. They need a proper APS and probably a stronger planner."
- On the footprint review: "I have to explain sixty-nine thousand tonnes of empty capacity to the board in June. Every plant manager knows it."
- On AI: "I am in favor, if it runs on the shop floor and not in a slide."
- He did not have a view on where variable costs are highest by plant; he referred us to Mireille.

## Our notes
- The 51% at Haguenau is computed against calendar time since 2023 (see [finance/pnl-2025-haguenau](../finance/pnl-2025-haguenau.md)). To be checked with Nathalie Klein.
