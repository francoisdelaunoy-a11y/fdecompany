## Context
CFO since 2017. Forty-five minutes, with her plant controller for Lyon, Julien, for the last quarter of an hour.

## What she said
- Covenant: net debt €152M, EBITDA €48.3M in 2025, €43.5M in the 2026 budget. "Three point four nine. I need a margin, not a miracle."
- "Every capacity story I have heard since 2022 died in my P&L. The best example cost thirty-eight million: line H5 at Haguenau. It runs a third of the time and I pay its depreciation every month."
- "When the market shrinks, extra hours on a machine we already own are worth nothing. Show me a cost that leaves the P&L, or a sale we would otherwise lose."
- "A tonne we fail to deliver at Lyon costs me about €447 of contribution. A tonne of scrap we avoid saves about €1,037 of resin. Those are the two numbers I care about there."
- Inventory: "Seventy-eight days at Lyon. That is cash I would like back. But don't add it to the savings: cash is cash."
- On gains: "My controllers compute them. Not you. Agree the baseline with Julien in the first week."

## Numbers she gave
- EBITDA 2025 €48.3M; budget 2026 €43.5M; covenant 3.5x.
- Cost program €15M by end 2027, of which operations €6M "without an owner".
