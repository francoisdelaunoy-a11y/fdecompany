## What she said
- Contribution 27%, about €656 per tonne; automotive prices down 3% a year.
- Depreciation €9.8M, of which H5 €3.8M: "We pay it whether H5 runs or not."
- "About 80% of our conversion costs are fixed in the short term."
- "If we raised OEE by fifteen points tomorrow, we would make no more money. There are no orders to fill the hours. We would only spend more energy."
- Scrap 2.4%, about €2.4M of material a year: "Low, and stable."
