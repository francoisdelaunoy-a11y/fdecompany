# Polymex Industries: engagement knowledge bundle

Polymex Industries is a fictional, 102-year-old, family-owned plastics compounding group with four plants. This bundle holds everything the diagnostic team collected between 13 and 24 April 2026: the board memo, the 2025 P&L of the group and of each plant, capacity, capex, the bank covenant, sales history and plans, lost orders, the lean program, and 28 interviews. Every interview was recorded with the consent of the person interviewed; recordings stay on Polymex's tenant and the summaries below were read back to each person.

Rules: one fact in one place; every number links to its source; nothing about a person that we would not show them. Add your findings as new concepts, and a dated line to `log.md`.
