## What he said
- "Everybody has heard about the review in June. People think Lyon is too old and Haguenau too empty."
- "We will support anything that keeps the site alive and the jobs here."
- "We will oppose any tool that watches individual operators. Machines, yes. People, no."
- "Inform us before, not after. That is the law, and it is also how you keep people with you."
