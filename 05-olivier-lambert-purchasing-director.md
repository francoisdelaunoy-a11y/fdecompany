## What he said
- Materials are €310M, 60% of group revenue. Contracts are indexed on polymer price indices with a fixed adder.
- His share of the cost program, €6M, comes from renegotiated adders and consolidated suppliers. "That is signed or close to it."
- "Scrap is my resin on the floor. Lyon throws away 4.2% of what I buy for it, Haguenau 2.4%, Évreux 1.8%. Tarragona says 2.9%, but nobody really knows."
- At Lyon, scrap is reported net of regrind: "What they can regrind goes back in. What is left is a real loss."
- He cannot negotiate scrap away: "I buy cheaper. The plants decide how much of it they waste."
