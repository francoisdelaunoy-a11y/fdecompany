## What she said
- "We are a hundred and two years old. We survived because we changed before we had to. My grandfather sold the celluloid business in 1962, when everyone said it was our soul."
- "The family is patient. The bank is not."
- Lyon is "the heart of the company, where it started". She does not want sentiment to decide the footprint review: "But I want it decided on numbers that our own people believe."
- On AI: "Antoine says it can change our costs. I want to see it in one plant before we talk about four."
